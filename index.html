About Me

Harman Singh is a dual-title PhD candidate in geography and climate science at Pennsylvania State University (Penn State). She earned her master’s and bachelor’s in geography from Penn State. Her research focuses on inland urban flooding, disaster risk reduction, and water resource management. She has also been part of interdisciplinary teams researching transboundary water sharing in India, extreme events in the U.S., and the role of news media in solar and fracking infrastructure in Pennsylvania.
